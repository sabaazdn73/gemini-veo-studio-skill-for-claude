# Privacy

Gemini Veo Studio Skill for Claude has no server and collects nothing.

- **What leaves your computer:** the prompts and any images you choose (first/last frame, reference images) are sent from your machine directly to Google's Gemini API, using your own API key, to generate the video. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that request: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
- **What the skill stores:** only the files it writes in your working folder (the .mp4 and a .json with the request and the video URI). Nothing is sent to the author or any third party other than Google.
- **Your API key:** read from your own environment. It is never logged, written to disk or sent anywhere except Google's API.
- **Children:** the skill is not directed at people under 18.
